# Quality and acceptance

| Gate | Evidence required | Failure response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technical | Dimensions, file integrity, label/geometry, masks, reflections, compression and video timing | Correct the defect and issue a new revision |
| Creative | Named reviewer accepts the message, product hierarchy, skin/material realism and placement | Rework the idea or execution; do not upgrade a technical pass |
| Rights | Sources, model/music/voice/likeness use and claim permissions are documented | Hold affected assets or produce an approved alternative |
| Client | Exact selected files are accepted for the specified use | Keep review status and record requested changes |

Product identity is a veto: wrong variant, capacity text, logo, silhouette or cap cannot be accepted for faithful product representation. Compare at 100 percent size and at actual mobile placement size. Do not merge incompatible packaging references into an invented SKU.

For advertising, state a verified benefit. An attractive product arrangement is not proof of efficacy. AI people, simulated application and synthetic skin cannot be presented as a real testimonial or measured before/after result. Obtain approval for objective product claims.

For video, measure streams with a media probe: frame count, duration, native resolution, delivery resolution and frame rate. Inspect opening, middle and closing frames plus playback for temporal drift, flicker, label movement and loop continuity. An upscale is not native generation. A zoom on a still is a motion study, not a filmed demonstration.

Rights decisions apply to specific assets and intended distribution. An unpaid preview or subscription entitlement is not automatically a commercial licence. Remove or replace uncleared audio in client delivery. Keep its source privately if needed for the audit.

The CLI binds gates to selected output hashes. It does not inspect images, validate legal rights or measure ad performance; those checks require people and suitable tools.
